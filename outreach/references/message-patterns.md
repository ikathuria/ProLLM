# Message patterns

Structures, not scripts. Always fill with real, specific details. Never invent them.

**Referral ask (warm contact)**
1. Connection point (shared employer/school/past conversation).
2. The specific roles (job IDs or links), and one line on why the user fits.
3. The ask, framed so "no" is easy: "Would you be open to referring me, or pointing me to the right person?"
4. Make it forwardable: offer a resume link and a 2-line blurb.

**Referral ask (cold, same company)**
Same, but lead with something specific about their team or work, and ask for a 10-minute
chat or advice before asking for a referral.

**Reply to a recruiter**
Answer their question first. Restate interest in one line, give the facts they need
(availability, authorization, per the profile), propose a next step with times.

**Follow-up (no reply after ~7 days)**
Two to three lines, adds something new (an application ID, a relevant project), no guilt.

**Thank-you / after a chat**
Within 24h; one specific thing you learned; the agreed next step.

**Checklist before handing over a draft**
- Under the length limit for its channel.
- Exactly one ask.
- Every fact matches PROFILE.md (especially work authorization and dates).
- Nothing from the profile's "Never say" list.
- Person's name, company and role spelled as in what the user pasted.
