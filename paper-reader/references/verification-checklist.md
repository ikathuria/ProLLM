# Verification Checklist

Re-open the extracted text for each item — do not verify from memory of your own draft.

- [ ] Title, authors, year, version match the source front matter
- [ ] Every number in the notes appears in the source at the cited locator, identical digits and units
- [ ] Every quotation is verbatim (search the extracted text for it)
- [ ] Every table/figure/section locator exists and says what's attributed to it
- [ ] Baselines and datasets named are the ones actually in the paper
- [ ] Hedging preserved (may/suggests/we hypothesize not turned into certainty)
- [ ] All interpretation is labeled [Interpretation]
- [ ] Missing info is marked "Not stated" rather than filled in
- [ ] No content drawn from prior knowledge of the paper without a fetched source

If any check fails: fix it, then re-run the failed checks.
